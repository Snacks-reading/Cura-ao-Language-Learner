Quality Pass Before Upload

Use: Curaçao/Bonaire Papiamentu style where possible. This page is a travel rehearsal draft and should be labeled pending native-speaker review before public/native upload.

Replace risky lines:

  • Use Nos tin kambernan konektá? instead of a more literal connecting-room construction.
  • Use Nos ta bin selebrá di diesun aña di mi yiu muhé. for “we are visiting to celebrate my daughter’s 11th birthday.”
  • Use Nos por kana bai einan, òf nos mester bai ku outo? for “is it walkable or should we drive?”
  • Use accents on common verbs where helpful: rekomendá, selebrá, haña.
  • Use E ta sigur pa mucha? instead of fragment-style “safe for children?”
  • Use Nos por sinta pafó? for “Can we sit outside?”

Episode 1: Arrival

Welcome. This is not just a language lesson. This is a travel rehearsal. You have just landed in Curaçao. The plane door opens. Warm Caribbean air enters the cabin. Listen: Bon dia. Pause. Repeat now: Bon dia. Bon dia means good morning. At immigration, if asked why you are visiting, say: Nos ta bin selebrá di diesun aña di mi yiu muhé. This means: We are visiting to celebrate my daughter's 11th birthday. You leave the airport and need a taxi. Mi ta buska un taxi. I am looking for a taxi. Kuantu e ta kosta? How much does it cost? At the resort: Mi tin un reservashon. I have a reservation. Nos tin kambernan konektá? Do we have connecting rooms? Bo por yuda mi? Can you help me? Nos ta selebrá cumpleaño di mi yiu Riley. We are celebrating my daughter Riley's birthday. Nos por kana bai einan, òf nos mester bai ku outo? Can we walk there, or should we go by car? Tin parkeo serka? Is there parking nearby? Dinner begins. You open the wine list. Mi ta buska un bon Cabernet Sauvignon. I am looking for a good Cabernet Sauvignon. Bo tin Caymus? Do you have Caymus? Kua Cabernet bo ta rekomendá? Which Cabernet do you recommend? Dessert: Kiko postre bo tin awe? What desserts do you have today? Bo tin gelato? Do you have gelato? Bonus evening phrase: Lo tin fuegos artificiales anochi? Will there be fireworks tonight? Take a deep breath. You are ready for Curaçao.
